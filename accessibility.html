Accessibility statement

Last updated: 1 September 2026

Northline Yoga LLC wants this site to be usable by everyone, including people who navigate by keyboard, use a screen reader, magnify the page, or rely on high-contrast settings. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at Level AA.

What we have done

  • A skip-to-content link at the top of every page.
  • One h1 per page and headings in order, so screen reader users can navigate by structure.
  • Descriptive alternative text on every photograph.
  • Text and background colours checked for contrast, including on the dark slate bands.
  • Body text at 17px with a line height of 1.65, and no text below 16px on mobile.
  • Visible focus outlines on every link, button and form field.
  • A mobile menu and FAQ accordion that work with the keyboard and report their open state with aria-expanded. Escape closes the menu.
  • Tap targets of at least 44 by 44 pixels.
  • Form fields with real labels, not placeholder text standing in for one.
  • No autoplaying media, no pop-ups, no interstitials, no auto-refresh and no motion effects.
  • Layout that reflows without horizontal scrolling down to a 360px viewport, and that survives zooming to 200%.

How the site is built

This is a plain static site: hand-written HTML, one stylesheet, and a single small JavaScript file that does two jobs. Nothing on the page is generated by a framework, and no component library sits between the markup and what a screen reader reads out. That is a deliberate accessibility decision as much as a technical one, because it means the heading order, the link text and the form labels are exactly what they appear to be in the source.

If JavaScript fails to load, the site still works. On narrow screens the header menu will not open without it, so the same set of links is repeated as plain text links in the footer of every page, which is where they remain reachable. The FAQ answers stay collapsed in that case, but every one of them repeats information that also appears on the classes or guide pages, so no content exists only inside an accordion.

Known limitations

  • Advertising served by third parties is outside our control. Its markup, contrast and focus behaviour are set by the advertiser and the ad network, not by us. We label every ad area so it is at least identifiable.
  • Photographs are decorative in some places. Their alt text describes what is shown but does not add information beyond the surrounding text.
  • Colour contrast on photographs themselves has not been adjusted; the images are used unmodified.

How we check

Before a page goes up we run through the same short list: tab through it from the top with no mouse and confirm every link, button and field can be reached and used; check that the visible focus indicator never disappears; measure text and background colour pairs against the 4.5:1 threshold, including on the dark slate bands; view it at 360 pixels wide and at 200% zoom to confirm nothing scrolls sideways or overlaps; and read the headings in order to check the outline still makes sense on its own. This is manual checking by one person rather than a formal third-party audit, so treat this statement as an honest account of our effort rather than a certification.

Telling us about a problem

If any part of this site is difficult or impossible for you to use, email hello@northlineyoga.com with the subject line Accessibility. It helps if you can tell us the page address, what you were trying to do, and what browser and assistive technology you were using — but send it even if you cannot, because a short message is much better than none.

We aim to reply within two business days and to fix confirmed problems as quickly as we reasonably can. If we cannot fix something straight away, we will tell you what the timeline is and offer the content another way.

You can also write to us: Northline Yoga LLC, 1420 Lakeshore Avenue, Suite 210, Duluth, MN 55802, United States. Other ways to reach us are on the contact page.
