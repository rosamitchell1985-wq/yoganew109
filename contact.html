Response window

We aim to reply within two business days, Monday to Friday, US Central Time. Messages sent over a weekend or a US public holiday are usually answered on the next working day. If a week goes by with no reply, check your spam folder and then send the message again — occasionally one does not arrive.

Requests about your data

To see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, email the address above with the subject line Data request and use the same email address you signed up with. We respond to verifiable requests within 45 days. Details of what we collect are in the privacy policy.

What we can help with

  • Which class to start with, given what you already do
  • Substitutes for a block, a strap or a chair
  • Starter plan emails that have not arrived
  • Unsubscribing, or correcting the name on your signup
  • Accessibility problems with this website
  • Typos, broken links and anything else that looks wrong here

What we cannot help with

We cannot assess an injury, tell you whether a movement is safe for your particular back or knee, or advise on a diagnosed condition. That is a conversation for your physician or physical therapist, who can actually look at you. If you write in describing pain, that is what we will tell you, so please save yourself the wait and go straight there.

If you are experiencing chest pain, severe shortness of breath, or sudden numbness or weakness, do not email us. Call 911 or your local emergency number.

Getting a faster answer

Most of what arrives in the inbox is already written down somewhere on this site, and the answer on the page is usually longer and more useful than the one we can type on a Tuesday afternoon. Before you write, it is worth a look at the classes page, which lists the equipment each session needs and who it suits, and the practice guide, which covers how many sessions a week to aim for and how to fit them around lifting, running or swimming.

If you do write, one detail speeds everything up: tell us what else you already do. “I lift four days a week and my hips are the limit on squats” gets a specific answer. “Which class should I start with?” gets a follow-up question and another two-day wait.

For anything to do with the emails themselves — a plan that stopped arriving, a name spelled wrong, an unsubscribe that did not seem to take — write from the address you signed up with if you can. It saves us asking, and it is how we confirm the request is really coming from you.
