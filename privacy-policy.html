Privacy policy

Last updated: 1 September 2026

This policy explains what Northline Yoga LLC (“Northline Yoga”, “we”, “us”) collects when you use www.northlineyoga.com, why we collect it, who else can see it, and what you can do about it. It applies to this website only.

1. Who we are

Northline Yoga LLC, 1420 Lakeshore Avenue, Suite 210, Duluth, MN 55802, United States. For any privacy question or request, email hello@northlineyoga.com.

2. Information you give us

  • Email signup. If you request the free 7-day starter plan, we collect the email address you enter and, if you provide it, your first name.
  • Contact form and email. If you write to us, we hold your name, email address and whatever you choose to put in the message, so that we can reply and keep a record of the exchange.

We do not ask for and do not want health information, dates of birth, postal addresses or payment details through this site. Please do not send us medical details; we cannot act on them.

3. Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our hosting provider records technical information each time a page is requested: IP address, browser type and version, operating system, the page requested, the referring page and the time of the request. This is used to keep the site running, to diagnose faults and to detect abuse. Server logs are retained for a limited period and then deleted.

4. Cookies

We do not set cookies for advertising or analytics ourselves. Cookies may be set by third parties whose services appear on the site, as described below. Full detail, including how to control cookies in your browser, is on our cookie policy page.

5. Third-party vendors, including Google

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website and to other websites on the internet.

Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visit to this site and/or other sites on the internet.

Users may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting https://www.google.com/settings/ads. Alternatively, users may opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalised advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info. If you have not opted out of third-party ad serving, the cookies of other third-party vendors or ad networks may also be used to serve ads on this site.

Where advertising is served on this site, it is served by Google and its partners, and their handling of your data is governed by Google's own privacy terms for partner sites. Advertising slots on this site are clearly labelled “Advertisement”.

Other third parties that may receive a request from your browser when you load our pages:

  • Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com, fonts.gstatic.com), which serves the typefaces used on this site.
  • Pexels (images.pexels.com), which hosts the photographs shown on this site.
  • Our hosting provider, which serves the pages themselves.

These providers receive your IP address and basic request information as a technical necessity of delivering the file.

6. Why we process your information

To send the starter plan you asked for; to reply to messages you send us; to keep the website secure and working; and to meet our legal obligations. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share your email address with other companies for their own marketing.

7. How long we keep it

Email addresses on the mailing list are kept until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete them. Correspondence is kept for up to two years so we have context if you write again. Server logs are kept for a short period as described above.

8. Your choices

  • Unsubscribe from any email using the link at the bottom of it. This removes you from the list.
  • Access, correct or delete the information we hold. Email us with the subject line “Data request” from the address you signed up with.
  • Control cookies through your browser settings and through the Google and aboutads.info links above.

9. California residents (CCPA/CPRA)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to know what categories of personal information we have collected about you and the purposes for which it is used; to request a copy of that information; to request its deletion; to request correction of inaccurate information; and not to be discriminated against for exercising any of these rights.

Northline Yoga does not sell personal information and does not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising in exchange for money. Advertising served through Google and its partners may nonetheless involve the use of identifiers under California law; you can limit this using the opt-out links in section 5.

To exercise a California right, email hello@northlineyoga.com with the subject line “Data request”. We will verify your request by replying to the email address on file and will respond within 45 days. You may use an authorised agent; we will ask for written proof of their authority.

10. Children

This website is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children under 13 years of age. If you believe a child under 13 has given us information, email us and we will delete it.

11. Security

The site is served over HTTPS and access to the mailing list is limited to the people who need it. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

12. Links to other sites

Pages here link to sites we do not control, including Google, Pexels and the advertising opt-out pages named above. Their privacy practices are their own and this policy does not cover them.

13. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes we will update the “last updated” date at the top of the page. Material changes will also be noted in an email to the mailing list.

14. Contact

Northline Yoga LLC, 1420 Lakeshore Avenue, Suite 210, Duluth, MN 55802, United States — hello@northlineyoga.com.
